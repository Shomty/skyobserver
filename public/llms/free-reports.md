# Free Reports — Career, Personal, Daily

All three reports are **free**, **public**, and **do not require an account**. Each needs:

- Birth **date**
- Birth **time** (as precise as possible; unknown time reduces accuracy)
- Birth **place** (city search with geocoding for latitude/longitude/timezone)

Reports are generated on the server using chart calculation + Gemini interpretation. Results can be printed or shared via a link (`/career/r/:reportId`, etc.).

---

## Career path report

**URL:** https://thesoulblueprint.online/career

**Purpose:** Help users understand professional strengths, suitable work environments, and favorable timing for career moves.

**Typical sections:**

- Work temperament and natural strengths
- Roles and environments that fit
- Growth edges and friction points
- Timing windows — when to push forward vs consolidate
- Plain-language coaching tied to dasha/transit context

**Best for:** Career decisions, job changes, understanding work-related patterns.

---

## Personality blueprint

**URL:** https://thesoulblueprint.online/personal

**Purpose:** A calm snapshot of personality — how someone shows up outwardly vs inwardly, core motivations, and recurring life themes.

**Typical sections:**

- Personality wheel — thinking, feeling, relating styles
- Public face vs private needs
- Core motivations and blind spots
- Life mission / recurring themes
- Relationship and self-awareness notes

**Best for:** Self-understanding, therapy-adjacent reflection, relationship context.

---

## Daily energy report

**URL:** https://thesoulblueprint.online/daily

**Purpose:** Today's emotional weather plus a **7-day ahead** forecast personalized to the user's natal chart and current transits.

**Typical sections:**

- Today's mind, body, and mood tone
- Best timing windows for the day
- 7-day forecast — highs, lows, pacing
- Practical coaching for the week

**Best for:** Daily check-ins, planning the week, mood/energy awareness.

**Note:** Content is date-sensitive; regenerate for a new day rather than caching stale daily text.

---

## Shared report links

When a user shares a report, the URL looks like:

- `https://thesoulblueprint.online/career/r/{reportId}`
- `https://thesoulblueprint.online/personal/r/{reportId}`
- `https://thesoulblueprint.online/daily/r/{reportId}`

These are **user-generated content**, not static documentation. Treat birth details embedded in shared reports as sensitive personal data.

---

## Limitations (disclose to users)

- Accuracy depends on birth time precision; rectification is not offered in free flow.
- Reports are reflective/coaching tools, not medical, legal, or financial advice.
- Sidereal positions differ from tropical sun-sign horoscopes.
